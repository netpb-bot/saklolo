Rescuer:

Principles

  1. Black is home. When the user isn't acting, the screen is off. The screen costs ~50× the beacon.
  2. Outlines, not fills. On OLED, every lit pixel costs power; white costs the most.
  3. Still frames. No looping animation, so the panel can drop its refresh rate and the GPU can sleep.
  4. Ears and hands first. Use haptics and sound for ACK, ring, the CPR beat and hot/cold.
  5. One fix, then stop. GPS, model and scans run in short bursts, not continuously.
  6. One thing at a time. Each screen shows one prompt; everything else waits.
